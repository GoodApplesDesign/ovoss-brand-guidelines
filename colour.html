Balance

Mostly white. Deep Navy for the things people need to read. Ice Blue and Soft Silver in small amounts, and the range colour smaller still.

60% White and Mist White 25% Deep Navy 10% Ice Blue and Soft Silver 5% Range colour

Range colours

Each range gets a dark and a light colour. On pack, the dark colour takes the place of Deep Navy on the solid blocks: the carton lid and base, and the active oxygen mark. The light colour replaces Ice Blue in the wave.

The logo and type stay Deep Navy on every range, to keep the family together.
