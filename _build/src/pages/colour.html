Range colours

Each range gets a dark and a light colour. On pack, the dark colour takes the place of Deep Navy on the solid blocks: the carton lid and base, and the dentist recommended mark. The light colour replaces Ice Blue in the wave.

The logo and type stay Deep Navy on every range, to keep the family together. Only the V takes the range colour: the dark one on white, the light one on the range’s dark colour.
