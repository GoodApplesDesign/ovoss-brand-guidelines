Overview

Ovoss is oral care made for people with dental implants. This site shows how the brand looks and how to use it.

Use it for anything with the Ovoss name on it, from packs to a flyer in the practice. If something isn’t covered here, check with Cuross Health before you start.

The brand

The name

The O stands for active oxygen, the technology in the toothpaste and mouthwash. OSS comes from osseointegration, the bond between an implant and the jaw.

The line

Science for oral longevity. It sits under the logo or at the end of a page.

Who it’s for

People with implants, and the dental practices that look after them. Practices recommend it, and patients reorder online.

How it’s organised

Ovoss is Cuross Health’s own brand. It has four ranges. Implant Care is first, and the other three follow.
