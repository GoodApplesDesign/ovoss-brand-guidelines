Brand guidelines

Packaging

Every pack follows the same layout, so the range reads as one family on the shelf and in the practice.

The renders come straight from the current print files, finishes included. Products still to come are marked.

Front panel

Seven parts, top to bottom, always in this order. Centre everything and keep the gaps generous.

  • 1 Logo lockupImplant Care lockup, Deep Navy, foil V, high build
  • 2 Product nameAvenir Next Demi Bold, spaced capitals
  • 3 DescriptorSerif italic, one line
  • 4 ClaimsUp to four, one per line, Claims Grey
  • 5 Dentist recommended markLine mark in foil, with high build
  • 6 VolumeAvenir Next Demi Bold
  • 7 The waveMist Wave with a foil trim, along the base

Finishes

Two finishes on every pack, printed after the CMYK: a metallic foil and a raised gloss called high build. They are what make the packs feel premium in the hand, so keep them on every product.

The logo

Foil V, high build on every letter.

The wave trim

Foil, with high build.

Dentist recommended mark

All foil, with high build.

Graphic elements

The wave

Mist Wave with a foil trim along the top, at the bottom of every pack. On the carton it runs all the way round and meets at the back corner. Never behind text, never at the top.

SVG
Dentist recommended mark

On the front of every pack. A line mark, all foil, with high build. Don’t redraw it.

Use-by and batch symbols

The standard symbols Ardoz needs: use within 12 months of opening, and the batch number.

Print notes

  • ColourCMYK, using the values on the Colour page, on a light tint
  • FinishesFoil and high build, each on its own spot layer (see Finishes)
  • TextOutlined in the print PDF
  • Barcodes80% size or larger, 100% black on white
  • Trade mark™ after OVOSS, not ®
  • Files to ArdozOn their dielines, as PDF/X-4
