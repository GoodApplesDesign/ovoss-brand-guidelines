Type

Two typefaces. Avenir Next does most of the work, and a serif italic adds a softer second voice.

They’re the same two used on the packs.

Avenir Next

Licence

For everything except range names and descriptors. It comes with every Mac and iPhone. On Windows, it’s in Microsoft 365 as Avenir Next LT Pro.

Noto Serif Italic

Free download

For range names and short descriptors, like Implant Care and with active oxygen. Always italic, never in capitals, never for long copy.

It’s the free, current version of Droid Serif, which the first brand guidelines used.

Hierarchy

Set it the way the packs are set. Capitals only for product names and headings, always spaced out. Everything else in sentence case.

Product name
Toothpaste
Avenir Next Demi Bold, capitals, tracking +220. 11 pt on the carton.

with active oxygen
Noto Serif Italic. 8.5 pt on the carton.

Low fluoride (200 ppm)

75 ml

Brush teeth, implants and gums thoroughly for two minutes, at least twice a day, using a soft toothbrush.
Avenir Next Regular. 5.6 pt minimum on pack.
