Brand guidelines

Logo

One wordmark and two lockups. Most of the time, the wordmark on its own is all you need.

Always use the supplied files. Never retype or redraw the logo.

Lockups

Use the wordmark unless you’re talking about a range or the brand as a whole.

Download
Wordmark

The default. Use it wherever the range is already clear, or doesn’t matter.

Download
Implant Care lockup

For the Implant Care range: packs, range pages and range ads.

Download
Brand line lockup

For the brand as a whole: the website footer, stationery and trade stands.

Keep the ™ after OVOSS until the trade mark is registered. Don’t use ®.

Colour

Deep Navy on light backgrounds, white on Deep Navy. Nothing else.

Deep Navy on whiteThe default, and what the packs use.

Clear space

Leave space the height of the O on every side. Nothing else goes in it, including the edge of the page.

Implant Care lockup

Minimum size

Don’t go smaller than this, or the ™ and the serif start to fill in.

  • Wordmark12 mm in print · 60 px on screen
  • Implant Care lockup15 mm in print · 72 px on screen
  • Brand line lockup25 mm in print · 120 px on screen
  • Measured acrossThe full width, ™ included

The panel shows each lockup at its minimum screen size.

Don’ts

Stretch or squash it
Tilt it
Recolour it
Use low contrast
Add shadows or effects
Outline it
Put it in a badge
Set it over the wave
