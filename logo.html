Brand guidelines

Logo

A wordmark, two lockups and a monogram. Most of the time, the wordmark on its own is all you need.

Always use the supplied files. Never retype or redraw the logo.

Lockups

Use the wordmark unless you’re talking about a range or the brand as a whole. The monogram is for small, square spaces.

Monogram

The O from the wordmark in a ring. For app icons, social avatars and the browser tab, where the brand is already clear.

Colour

Deep Navy on light backgrounds, white on Deep Navy. The V is the one letter that changes: on pack it’s foil. On screen it’s Foil Blue on light backgrounds and Mist Wave on Deep Navy.

On a range’s packs, the V takes that range’s foil.

Deep Navy on Mist TintThe default, and what the packs use.

Clear space

Leave space the height of the O on every side. Nothing else goes in it, including the edge of the page.

Minimum size

Don’t go smaller than this, or the ™ and the serif start to fill in.

  • Wordmark12 mm in print · 60 px on screen
  • Implant Care lockup15 mm in print · 72 px on screen
  • Brand line lockup25 mm in print · 120 px on screen
  • Monogram6 mm in print · 24 px on screen, 16 px as a browser icon
  • Measured acrossThe full width, ™ included

The panel shows each lockup at its minimum screen size.
